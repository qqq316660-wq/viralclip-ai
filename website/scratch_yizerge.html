Questions. Answered simply.

Everything you need to know before installing YIZERGE. Clean answers, zero marketing fluff.

  • YIZERGE Video Editor was conceived, engineered, and created by Sumant Kael, host of Late Night with Sumant Kael. Sumant built YIZERGE to solve the daily frustration video editors face when manually re-framing widescreen 16:9 recordings into vertical 9:16 shorts, providing a fast, 100% offline desktop tool without subscription paywalls.

  • YIZERGE is a video repurposing editor designed specifically for video editors, content creators, podcasters, and stream clippers. It turns 16:9 widescreen videos into viral 9:16 vertical shorts for TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels with speaker auto-framing, dynamic animated karaoke subtitles, and local GPU acceleration.

  • YIZERGE runs on Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit) and on macOS (Apple Silicon). It uses local hardware encoding: NVIDIA NVENC, AMD AMF, and Intel QuickSync on Windows, and VideoToolbox on Mac.

  • Never. YIZERGE works 100% offline. All video decoding, cropping, audio processing, and hardware encoding happen directly inside your computer's local CPU and GPU. You can edit and export on an airplane or without an internet connection.

  • You can import standard subtitle files (.srt or .ass), or type text directly into the headline overlay. The subtitle engine animates individual words with highlight colors, bounce transitions, drop shadows, and glow effects, automatically positioned above TikTok/Reels UI buttons.

  • YIZERGE accepts virtually every format: MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, and AVI. You can export in standard 1080×1920 (Full HD vertical) or 2160×3840 (4K Ultra HD) at 24fps, 30fps, or 60fps with crystal-clear bitrate presets.
